## Milestones

| Milestone | Target | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Charter and scope approved | End of week 2 | Approved boundary and requirements plan |
| Prototype reviewed | End of week 4 | Screen review and notes |
| Technical option selected for validation | End of week 5 | Option matrix and conditions |
| Pilot controls ready | End of week 7 | Test, risk, data, and recovery evidence |
| Sponsor decision recorded | End of week 8 | Go, no-go, or defer decision |

## Conditions to advance

- Scope must be clear before the prototype is treated as evidence.
- A technical option must be reviewed before cost and recovery estimates are final.
- Test and data controls must be ready before pilot-readiness review.
- The sponsor decision must be recorded before any pilot build or production work.

## Capacity view

The schedule assumes the part-time capacity in Document 14. Work can move inside a week when the next gate is not affected. The likely critical path is scope -> technical option -> tests and controls -> readiness evidence -> sponsor gate.

## Baseline control

The sponsor approves the initial eight-week baseline at the end of week 2. A change to scope, decision date, budget boundary, or pilot conditions must be recorded with its reason and impact before the baseline is changed.

## Key terms

**Baseline:** The approved plan used to track progress.

**Milestone:** A meaningful point or result in the plan.

**Critical path:** The chain of work that controls the final decision date.

**Change control:** The process for reviewing and approving a change to the plan.
