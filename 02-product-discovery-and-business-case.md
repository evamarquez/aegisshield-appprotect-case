# Document 02 - Product Discovery and Business Case

**Project:** AegisShield AppProtect  
**Stage:** Discovery  
**Status:** Draft for evaluation

## Business opportunity

AegisShield wants to evaluate a second product that complements its existing SIEM service.

AppProtect would inspect incoming requests to customer web applications and apply configured protection rules. Customers would manage the service through a dedicated administration panel.

## Customer hypothesis

The proposed target is businesses that operate web applications and have limited internal security capacity.

The hypothesis is that some customers would value application protection configured and supported by AegisShield. This has not yet been validated.

BookEasy is a proposed discovery participant and possible pilot customer. Participation does not imply a purchase commitment.

## Discovery questions

- What protection does the customer already use?
- Which problems remain unresolved?
- Who configures security rules and investigates incorrect blocks?
- What would justify purchasing another service?
- Who makes the purchasing decision, and what budget could support it?

## Alternatives to evaluate

- Continue using the customer's existing protection.
- Purchase or resell an established service.
- Build an AegisShield product around an existing security engine.
- Develop a proprietary engine. This option needs separate justification because of its technical and operational demands.

## Proposed value to test

AegisShield would offer application protection with guided setup, understandable event records, and support for investigating incorrect blocks.

These are proposed benefits, not established competitive advantages. Discovery must determine whether customers value them and whether existing products already meet their needs.

## Discovery activities

- Product leads customer interviews and competitor analysis.
- Engineering and Security evaluate technical options and protection limits.
- Design prepares a prototype of setup, event review, and rule management.
- Operations assesses hosting, monitoring, support, and recovery needs.
- The Project Manager coordinates activities, dependencies, estimates, and the sponsor review.

## Commercial and delivery assessment

The team estimates development costs and ongoing costs for infrastructure, licensing, security rule maintenance, and customer support.

Product proposes a pricing hypothesis. The team evaluates whether potential revenue could support those costs.

All figures used in this educational case must be identified as simulated assumptions.

## Decision criteria

Recommend a limited development stage only if discovery identifies a clear customer problem, a credible reason to choose AppProtect, a feasible technical approach, and an acceptable estimate of costs and risks.

Otherwise, recommend further investigation, a partnership, or stopping the initiative.

## Sponsor decision

Pending completion of discovery.

The decision record will identify the evidence reviewed, selected option, approved funding boundary, and conditions for the next stage.

## Key terms

**Business case:** The reasons, costs, expected benefits, and risks used to assess an investment.

**Customer hypothesis:** An assumption about customer needs that must be tested.

**Build versus buy:** A comparison between developing a solution and obtaining an existing one.

**Operating costs:** The ongoing cost of running and supporting the product.

**Decision gate:** A review where the sponsor decides whether and how work should continue.
