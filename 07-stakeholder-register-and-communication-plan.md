# Document 07 - Stakeholder Register and Communication Plan

**Project:** AegisShield AppProtect  
**Stage:** Product definition  
**Status:** Draft for kickoff confirmation

## Stakeholder register

| Stakeholder | Interest | Role in the case |
|---|---|---|
| CTO / Sponsor | Investment, risk, and product direction | Approves stage boundaries, budget assumptions, and decision gates. |
| Project Manager | Coordination and visibility | Maintains the plan, risks, decisions, and communication. |
| Product Lead | Customer value and priorities | Owns the product hypothesis, scope, and customer scenarios. |
| Engineering Lead | Feasibility and delivery effort | Assesses the engine, integration, architecture, and estimates. |
| Security Lead | Safe protection and data handling | Reviews rules, access, logging, privacy, and false-positive controls. |
| Product Designer | Usable administration experience | Designs the setup, event review, rule, and exception journeys. |
| Operations Lead | Service health and support | Defines monitoring, on-call, recovery, and handover needs. |
| QA Lead | Evidence and customer validation | Plans tests and records acceptance evidence. |
| Customer Success | Customer coordination | Prepares BookEasy communication and pilot readiness. |
| BookEasy representative | Practical customer needs | Reviews scenarios and accepts or rejects the pilot results. |
| Support | Customer questions and escalation | Confirms how incorrect blocks are reported and handled. |

## Communication plan

| Audience | What they need | Channel | Frequency | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sponsor | Progress, risks, decisions, cost and schedule changes | Written status summary | Weekly and at gates | Project Manager |
| Core team | Work, blockers, dependencies, and next actions | Working meeting | Twice weekly | Project Manager |
| BookEasy and Customer Success | Scenarios, test windows, expected impact, and results | Review call and email | Weekly during pilot preparation | Product / PM |
| Security and Operations | Controls, test evidence, alerts, and recovery | Review session | At design and release gates | Security / Operations |
| Support | Known limits, customer message, and escalation path | Handover session | Before pilot and handover | Customer Success |

## Escalation rules

- The Project Manager escalates a material scope, schedule, cost, or customer-impact risk to the sponsor.
- Security escalates unsafe data handling, access, or rule behavior immediately.
- Operations escalates service-health or recovery concerns before a pilot or release gate.
- Customer Success escalates a customer concern that could affect participation or acceptance.

## Key terms

**Stakeholder:** A person or group affected by the project or able to influence it.

**Communication plan:** The agreement for what information is shared, with whom, when, and how.

**Escalation:** Raising a problem to someone with the authority to decide or help.

**Decision gate:** A scheduled review where evidence is used to make a decision.
