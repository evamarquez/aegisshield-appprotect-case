# Document 14 - Budget and Capacity Plan

**Project:** AegisShield AppProtect  
**Stage:** Delivery planning  
**Status:** Simulated planning estimate

> All amounts and capacity figures are fictional assumptions for this portfolio case. They are not a quote or a real company budget.

## Capacity assumptions

| Role | Availability | Main focus |
|---|---:|---|
| Project Manager | 50% | Plan, risks, decisions, status, and coordination |
| Product Lead | 30% | Customer problem, scope, and value hypothesis |
| Engineering Lead | 40% | Engine review, technical path, and estimates |
| Security Lead | 20% | Rule safety, data, access, and audit controls |
| Product Designer | 35% | Journey and prototype |
| Operations Lead | 20% | Monitoring, support, recovery, and cost input |
| QA Lead | 25% | Scenarios, evidence, and readiness review |
| Customer Success | 15% | BookEasy participation and communication |

## Ongoing cost questions

- Engine license per application or request volume.
- Hosting and traffic costs.
- Monitoring and alerting costs.
- Rule maintenance and security review effort.
- Customer support and incident response coverage.
- Data retention and audit storage.

## Capacity risks

- Engineering and Security may be needed for the same engine review.
- BookEasy availability may affect customer validation.
- Operations may not have enough time to review recovery and support needs.
- Licensing information may arrive after the technical comparison starts.

## Budget decision boundary

The current estimate supports discovery and technical validation. It does not authorize production hosting, a full engineering build, or a customer rollout. The sponsor must approve a new funding boundary before the project moves into pilot build work.

## Key terms

**Capacity:** The time a role can give to the project.

**Blended rate:** A planning rate used to estimate a group of roles without naming individual pay rates.

**Contingency:** A reserve for uncertainty.

**Operating cost:** A cost that continues after the product is running.
