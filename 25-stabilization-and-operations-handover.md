# Document 25 - Stabilization and Operations Handover

**Project:** AegisShield AppProtect  
**Stage:** Controlled pilot build close-out  
**Status:** Simulated handover record

## Stabilization period

The team monitors the controlled pilot build for five business days. During this period, the default mode is monitor, the rule and exception audit is reviewed, and the support path is tested.

## Handover checklist

| Item | Receiving role | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Service overview and application ownership | Operations Lead | Accepted for controlled pilot support |
| Health view and alert route | Operations Lead | Reviewed and tested |
| Rule and exception process | Security Lead | Reviewed; expiry and approval required |
| Safe configuration restore | Engineering / Operations | Rehearsed successfully |
| Known limits | Product / Support | Recorded and shared |
| Customer support message | Customer Success / Support | Reviewed with BookEasy |
| Escalation path | Support / Security / Engineering | Confirmed |

## Known limitations

- The product is validated only for one BookEasy test application.
- Production performance is not proven by this case.
- The security engine and license terms require final confirmation.
- The prototype and simulated evidence do not prove real-world threat coverage.
- Block mode requires separate approval and more evidence.

## Handover decision

Operations accepts the service for the limited controlled pilot scope. This acceptance does not approve production rollout or a broader customer launch.

## Key terms

**Stabilization:** The period after a change when the team watches service health and resolves early issues.

**Handover:** Transfer of service knowledge and support responsibility to Operations.

**Escalation path:** The route for raising a problem to the right team.

**Known limitation:** A confirmed boundary that users must understand.
