## Responsibility notes

- The PM coordinates the work but does not replace technical or security ownership.
- The Sponsor makes the investment and stage decisions.
- Product owns customer value and scope priorities.
- Engineering owns the technical assessment and delivery estimate.
- Security owns safe rule behavior, data protection, access, and audit review.
- Operations owns monitoring, support, recovery, and handover readiness.
- BookEasy validates customer scenarios; it does not approve AegisShield internal design decisions.

## Key terms

**Responsible:** The role that performs the work.

**Accountable:** The role that answers for the result or makes the final decision.

**Consulted:** A person whose input is needed before the work is completed.

**Informed:** A person who receives an update but does not approve the work.
