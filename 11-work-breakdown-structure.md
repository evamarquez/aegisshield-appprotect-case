## Completion evidence

| WBS | Work package | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | Approved brief and charter | Sponsor-approved boundary and authority |
| 1.3 | Product scope and acceptance checklist | Requirements linked to customer scenarios |
| 2.2 | Visual prototype | Reviewable screens using simulated data |
| 3.1 | Security engine comparison | Option matrix and license questions |
| 3.3 | Cost and operating assessment | Simulated estimate with assumptions |
| 4.2 | Risk and recovery controls | Risk register and test plan |
| 4.3 | Pilot-readiness recommendation | Evidence summary and open conditions |
| 5.2 | Sponsor decision | Recorded approval, rejection, or deferral |
| 6.3 | Closure | Results, pending items, and lessons learned |

## Scope check

The WBS covers product definition, experience, technical validation, pilot planning, sponsor approval, and project management. It does not include a full production launch or a proprietary security engine.

## Key terms

**WBS:** A breakdown of the work into clear deliverables and work packages.

**Work package:** A group of work with a clear result and completion evidence.

**Deliverable:** Something the project produces and can hand over or review.

**Scope check:** A review to confirm that the WBS includes the approved work and excludes the rest.
